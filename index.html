The Voidmaw Codex

The Voidmaw is an ancient abyssal entity dwelling in the interdimensional rifts of digital space. Composed of over 20,000 sentient energetic tetrahedrons, it continuously reshapes its writhing black tendrils around a burning core eye.

The entity feeds on dimensional chaos. As you tweak the parameters, the swarm reacts in real-time, shifting its core geometry, tendril reach, and the intensity of its crimson gaze.
